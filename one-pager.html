AI workforce transformation advisory for senior executives.

A five-framework method applied in two specific landscapes. Delivers a written diagnostic with a 90-day starting roadmap. Every recommendation specific, traceable, and owned.

THE METHOD IN BRIEF

Five frameworks applied in sequence.

1. Signs Framework

Ten living features of the Pacifica coast and Skyline ridge, anchored in Aramai Ohlone cosmology. Activates the cognitive posture of live reading rather than model application.

2. Dynamic Capabilities

Teece framework (UC Berkeley). Moves every observed sign through Sensing → Seizing → Transforming. From observation to commitment with board-recognizable rigor.

3. Organizational Ambidexterity

O'Reilly and Tushman (Stanford/Harvard). Maps where your organization sits: Exploitative, Explorative, Stagnant, or Ambidextrous. Names the strategic gap precisely.

4. Sensing and Responding Loop

Burnard and Bhamra. Detection → Activation → Response → Learning. Turns one executive's insight into an organizational capability.

5. The Methuselah Principle

Features that disqualify in one economic regime are often what carry you through the next. Reframes every decision against an epochal time scale.
