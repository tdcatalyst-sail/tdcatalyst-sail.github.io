The Adaptability Imperative in AI Transformation

The Problem
Most organizations entering AI transformation are asking the wrong question. They ask: "How do we deploy AI effectively?" The more consequential question is: "What kind of leadership does AI make necessary?" — and whether their people are capable of it.

As AI absorbs the performance layer of work, what remains irreducibly human is the adaptability layer: reading complex, ambiguous signals in real time and acting with complete ownership before the competition has noticed they exist. This capacity does not develop in conference rooms. It develops in environments that demand it.

The Framework

Five Disciplines for Adaptive Leadership

The method draws on Dynamic Capabilities (Teece, UC Berkeley), Organizational Ambidexterity (O'Reilly & Tushman, Harvard/Stanford), and Sensing and Responding (Burnard & Bhamra). These frameworks have been tested against real enterprise transformation contexts, not constructed for a slide deck.
