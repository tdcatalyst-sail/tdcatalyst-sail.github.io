Privacy

How this site handles your information.

TDcatalyst collects the minimum it needs to respond to you, uses no cookies and no analytics, and never sells or shares personal data. This page says exactly what is collected, where it goes, and how to have it removed.

Effective July 10, 2026.

Who we are

TDcatalyst is the advisory practice of Tom Delaporte, based in Pacifica, California. For anything related to this policy or your personal data, write to sail@tdcatalyst.com.

What we collect, and where it goes

Contact forms. The forms on this site (Begin, Grow, Advise) ask for your name, email, and optionally your company, role, and a message. Submissions are relayed by FormSubmit, a third-party form service, and delivered to us by email. We use them solely to respond to your inquiry.

The AI Transformation Stall Diagnostic. Your answers are processed in your browser. If you choose to share your contact details at the end, your name, email, company, department, your responses, and basic technical context (browser user agent and referring page) are transmitted to us and stored in our Google Workspace account. We use this to prepare and send your diagnostic readout and to follow up if you ask us to. If you complete the diagnostic without sharing contact details, we receive the responses without any identifying information beyond that technical context.

The Transformation Dynamics Simulation™ runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is transmitted or stored.

Scheduling. Booking a call happens on Cal.com, under Cal.com's own privacy policy.

Chat. One page hosts a chat widget provided by Asksila. Conversations you have with it are processed by that service.

Technical delivery. Like most websites, this site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a small number of images from external image services. Serving those files necessarily exposes your IP address to those providers. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log requests for operational purposes.

What we do not do

This site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, uses no tracking pixels, and carries no advertising. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with anyone, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

Retention

Correspondence and diagnostic submissions are kept for as long as they are relevant to an ongoing or prospective conversation, and deleted when they no longer are — or immediately on your request.

Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Email sail@tdcatalyst.com and we will act on the request promptly. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, these requests are honored in line with the GDPR; if you are a California resident, in line with the CCPA.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated effective date. The substance — minimal collection, no tracking, no selling — is not going to change.
