THE OFFER AT A GLANCE

AI workforce transformation advisory for senior executives.

A five-framework method applied in two specific landscapes. Delivers a written diagnostic with a 90-day starting roadmap. Every recommendation specific, traceable, and owned.

TDcatalyst advises the leaders working the seams between AI product, adoption/change agility, and people management. The work happens alongside you — providing the language, framing, and actions that build your organization's adaptability.

WHO IS IT FOR

Target audience.

  • VP / C-suite AI, Transformation, or Workforce leads
  • Business unit or department leadership in a large company
  • Accountability for how AI enters the operating model
  • Mid to large enterprises, any industry
WHY THE LANDSCAPE MATTERS

The pace of a walk changes what leaders notice.

The engagement is conducted outdoors on the Pacifica coast or Skyline ridge—not as retreat, but as diagnostic instrument. Distance from the conference room activates a different cognitive posture. Leaders say things on a trail they would not say in a boardroom. The landscape itself—reading ten living features anchored in 3,000 years of Aramai Ohlone observation—trains the eye and ear for signal detection. This is the posture AI workforce transformation requires: live reading rather than model application. Adaptability emerges from environments that demand it, not from seminars about it.

WHAT YOU GET

Four concrete deliverables from every engagement.

Every engagement concludes with a structured written diagnostic delivered within 7 to 10 days. The format and depth vary by tier. The four areas are consistent across all tiers.

01

Named Adaptive Challenge

A precise, written statement of the gap between your organization's current posture and the leadership capability the AI transition demands. Specific to your organization, built from your data. You understand exactly where to focus development.

02

Organizational Landscape Analysis

A structured read of the signals, assets, and constraints your operating environment is already producing. What your landscape is showing you, and what it is obscuring. How to read the seam where AI capability meets organizational readiness.

03

Leverage Inventory

The specific capabilities, structural advantages, and resources available to close the identified gap. An honest accounting of what is present, what is absent, and what is misallocated. Where your organization can move first.

04

90-Day Starting Roadmap

A sequenced set of first moves with clear ownership and decision points. Built so you can act before the signal hardens. Delivered as a flexible set of documents, slides, and frameworks tailored to your organization.
