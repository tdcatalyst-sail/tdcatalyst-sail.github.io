The Catalyst Method™

Five frameworks that hold under AI workforce change.

The Signs Framework™ activates the posture. Four strategic frameworks turn what the executive sees into organizational action. The sequence matters. Running the frameworks out of order produces weaker output. Skipping any of them produces a partial diagnosis.

Why the method exists

Most strategy frameworks assume clear signals and ask how to execute. AI workforce transformation does not produce clear signals. It produces ambiguity, weak signs, and competing interpretations. The standard playbooks were not built for that condition.

The Catalyst Method is a sequence built for that condition. A specific landscape activates a specific cognitive posture. Four strategy frameworks then turn that posture into diagnosis, commitment, infrastructure, and a reflection of the long horizon.

The Core Distinction

The adaptability layer remains irreducibly human.

As AI absorbs the performance layer of work (the execution, the optimization, the speed), what remains irreducibly human is the adaptability layer: the capacity to read complex, ambiguous signals in real time and act on them with complete ownership before the competition has noticed they exist.

Adaptability lives in your leaders' ability to hold two futures at once. It lives in your managers' capacity to have the difficult conversation with a direct report about what AI means for their role. It lives in your organization's willingness to let frontline people shape how adoption happens on their teams, not just comply with how it was designed.

The method is built to activate this posture through a specific sequence, on real ground, where the ambiguity your organization faces becomes the material for learning. Training modules and abstractly applied frameworks do not reach this posture.

02

Dynamic Capabilities

Producesdiagnosis to commitmentOriginTeece

Three capabilities, each doing different work. AI transformation fails most often at the third muscle: organizations that sense and seize without transforming end up with pilots that never make it into operating rhythm.

01 Sensing

Detects risks and opportunities before competitors.

02 Seizing

Commits resources to act on the signal once detected.

03 Transforming

Reconfigures the organization’s resources and structures to deliver on what was seized.

Inside the Catalyst Method, every sign observed during a field engagement receives a Teece cycle. The framework moves the executive from observation to commitment, with rigor a board will recognize.

Worked example, from a real engagement

A hawk works thermals above the coast. The sign surfaces the question: when did you last gain altitude on your own operation?

01 Sensing

No full day away from operational meetings in six months. Altitude already lost.

02 Seizing

A monthly day blocked for ridge-scale thinking. A quarterly off-site built on signal-reading, not planning.

03 Transforming

The calendar, the team structure, and the performance metrics that make altitude a first-class activity rather than a stolen one.

Organizational Ambidexterity

Producesthe explore/exploit agendaOriginO’Reilly & Tushman

The core tension. Exploitation runs the current business model with discipline; exploration reads signals and experiments. Doing both at once is rare because it is structurally difficult: the two modes require different cultures, metrics, leadership styles, and risk postures.

In an engagement, the executive places their current organization on the matrix. The placement is rarely flattering: most mid-to-large enterprises sit firmly in the Exploitative quadrant and have been rewarded for being there. The gap to the Ambidextrous quadrant becomes the strategic agenda.

Exploration high · Exploitation low Explorative

Reads signals well, struggles to monetize. Burns cash faster than it learns.

Exploration high · Exploitation high Ambidextrous

The posture AI workforce transformation requires. Rare. Demands structural design, not willpower.

Exploration low · Exploitation low Stagnant

Existential risk. Neither running the business well nor reading what is coming.

Exploration low · Exploitation high Exploitative

The default of most mature enterprises. Executes the current model well. Blind to paradigm shift.

The diagnostic does not resolve the gap in a single day. It names it precisely.

04

Sensing and Responding Loop

Producesan organizational capabilityOriginBurnard & Bhamra

Four phases that turn signal into practice. Without the fourth, every response is a one-off; with it, the organization compounds adaptive capacity over time. Inside a Catalyst Immersive engagement, the loop becomes a concrete organizational design question: who reads weak signals and how their time is protected, what the escalation path is, who decides whether a response is warranted and at what cost threshold.

01 Detection

Information about the operating environment flows in through deliberate channels.

02 Activation

A detected signal triggers a response process via decision rights and escalation paths.

03 Response

The organization acts: investment, repositioning, experiment, or explicit non-response.

04 Learning

The response is evaluated. Insight is captured and built into updated practice. Feeds back to Detection.

The first three frameworks produce a single executive’s insight. The loop produces an organizational capability.

05

The Methuselah Principle

Producesthe long-horizon reframeGroundthe redwood grove

Origin: Named for the roughly 1,800-year-old redwood on Skyline Boulevard, spared by 19th-century loggers because its burls and knobs made the timber commercially unworkable.

The principle. Features that disqualify an organization from one economic regime are often the features that carry it through to the next. Every executive leading AI transformation is being told to smooth out exactly the kind of characteristic that may be the thing the organization most needs to protect.

This framework comes at the end of every full-day or Immersive engagement. Standing at the base of the tree, every decision made during the day is reframed against an epochal time scale. The other frameworks move fast. The redwood slows everything down to the time scale that AI transformation actually operates on.

Which of today’s decisions will look obviously wrong in twenty years. Which features the organization is being told to smooth out may be the ones that carry it through.
Why the sequence matters

Five frameworks. One sequence.

Each framework does work the others cannot. They form a sequence rather than a pile: running them out of order produces weaker output, and skipping any of them produces a partial diagnosis.
