Sessions and Pathway

Four tiers. One methodology. Specific landscapes.

Every tier applies the full Catalyst Method. What differs is depth of engagement, number of field sessions, and the specificity of the organizational design work produced.

What every engagement produces

A written diagnostic. Four areas. Every finding specific.

Every engagement concludes with a structured written output delivered within 7 to 10 days. The format and depth vary by tier. The four areas are consistent across all tiers.

01

Named adaptive challenge

A precise, written statement of the gap between your organization's current posture and the leadership capability the AI transition demands. Specific to your organization, built from your data.

02

Organizational landscape analysis

A structured read of the signals, assets, and constraints your operating environment is already producing. What your landscape is showing you, and what it is obscuring.

03

Leverage inventory

The specific capabilities, structural advantages, and resources available to close the identified gap. An honest accounting of what is present, what is absent, and what is misallocated.

04

90-day starting roadmap

A sequenced set of first moves with clear ownership and decision points. Built so you can act before the signal hardens. Delivered within 7 to 10 days via Google Drive as a flexible set of documents, slides, and frameworks tailored to your organization.

Every finding tied to what we saw. Every move built for your situation.

How to choose a tier

Three prescriptive starting points.

Start with the Trail Diagnostic to evaluate the methodology or work a single near-term decision; choose the Catalyst Field Session for a full-arc diagnostic in one day when the challenge is clear; move to the Catalyst Immersive when the transformation will unfold over months and requires the depth of organizational design work a single engagement cannot produce.

Logistics

What to expect before, during, and after.

Location and meeting points

Engagements on the Pacifica coast take place at Mori Point, Pedro Point Headlands, or Pedro Point Valley depending on the engagement — all within Pacifica, approximately 25 minutes south of San Francisco International Airport. Precise meeting point confirmed at booking. For Field Session and Immersive, the afternoon portion is at the Methuselah grove on Skyline Boulevard, 25 minutes east of Pacifica.

Before the engagement

A 15-minute intake questionnaire one week before, followed by a 30-minute prep call the same week. The questionnaire surfaces the specific adaptive challenge and the context the day will work inside. Preparation is light by design.

During the engagement

Walks are deliberately slow. Anyone comfortable with a 3-mile walk on varied terrain can complete the day. Phones and laptops remain in the car during field portions. Emergency reachability is arranged in advance. Engagements proceed in fog, light rain, and typical coastal conditions. Heavy rain or high-wind warnings trigger a reschedule at no fee.

After the engagement

Written diagnostic delivered within 7 to 10 days via shared Google Drive. A flexible set of interconnected documents, slides, and frameworks—shorter modular pieces filled with insights, models, and diagnostic specificity. Optional 45-minute review call for Field Session and Immersive to walk through the artifacts and discuss next moves.
