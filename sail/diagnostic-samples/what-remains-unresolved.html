What the Engagement Did Not Resolve

Closing page of the diagnostic · Illustrative sample

A field engagement produces a diagnosis and a starting point. Listing what it did not produce belongs in the diagnostic, because an unmarked limit turns into a quiet overclaim.

The ambidexterity gap is named and open

Placing the organization in the exploitative quadrant took one honest conversation. Reaching the ambidextrous quadrant is structural design work: separate metrics, cultures, and leadership styles for the explore side, held together in one organization. The ninety days begin that work. They do not finish it.

The margin experiments are identified, and the call on them is pending

Whether to fund the margin operators as a formal explore cell, fold their methods into the core, or leave them protected where they are depends on [what the first weekly signal readings show]. Deciding now would substitute a default for evidence.

The identity work is opened and the threshold is uncrossed

The transition now has a name at the leadership table. The wider organization has not crossed it with them. The threshold work that carries the rest of the workforce through the passage sits outside the scope of a single field day and needs its own design.

Signs unseen

The Signs Framework is a scaffold. The day showed what it showed, several of the ten signs went unread, and a later walk would read differently. The diagnosis rests on what the landscape actually presented, which is its strength and its limit.

The 30-day check-in reviews whether the roadmap is holding. Where an item above becomes the work itself, that work runs at a different scale, and this page exists to have named it honestly. The next step is the first move on the roadmap, this week.
