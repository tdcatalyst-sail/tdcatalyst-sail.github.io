Starting Roadmap: The First 90 Days

Section 04 of 04 · First moves, sequenced, with ownership and decision points · Illustrative sample
Weeks Move Owner Decision point
1 1 – 2 Name the transition at the leadership table, including what the organization is moving away from. Block the monthly altitude day on the executive calendar. Chief executive The transition has one name every leader uses, or the gap routes to a dedicated leadership session.
2 2 – 3 Set the operating baseline for the two highest-volume roles: how the work is actually done today. Operations lead Baseline recorded as [from your week-one data].
3 3 – 5 Run the task-level analysis on those roles. Separate what AI absorbs from what requires human judgment, trust, and accountability. Operations lead, with people lead Proceed to redesign, or extend the analysis by one week.
4 5 – 7 Redesign the roles before any retraining: tasks, decisions, handoffs. Sanction the margin operators as the first explore cell. People lead Redesigned roles approved. The explore cell gets its own metrics.
5 7 – 9 Design the sensing loop: who reads which signals, at what cadence, with what authority to escalate, at what cost threshold. Named signal owner Loop on paper and the first weekly reading held.
6 9 – 11 Run one live decision through the full cycle: detection, activation, response, and a written learning capture. Chief executive Response taken, or explicit non-response recorded with reasons.
7 11 – 13 Review against the week-one baseline. Hold the quarter's decisions against the long horizon. Leadership team Continue, adjust, or rescope. Findings feed the 30-day check-in.
