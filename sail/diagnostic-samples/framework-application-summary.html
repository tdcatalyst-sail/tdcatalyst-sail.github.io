Framework Application Summary

How the five frameworks of the Catalyst Method were applied on the day · Illustrative sample

01Signs Framework

The walk opened at Mori Point with attention directed to the living features of the landscape. Three of the ten signs did the day's work. The red-tailed hawk carried the altitude question: when the executive last gained altitude on their own operation. Coyote surfaced where value is being created at the margins, outside the sanctioned processes. The fog held the question of what the organization keeps trying to clear rather than learning to navigate. The framework predicted none of this. It activated live reading, and the day followed what the landscape actually showed.

02Dynamic Capabilities

Each observed sign received a Teece cycle: what was sensed, what committing to it would require, and what the organization would have to reconfigure to deliver on the commitment. The cycle exposed the pattern that shaped the whole diagnostic. Sensing and seizing function well enough. Transforming is where the organization stalls, and the stall is structural.

03Organizational Ambidexterity

The executive placed the organization on the matrix. It landed in the exploitative quadrant, which was not flattering and is normal for a mature, well-run enterprise. The gap between that placement and the ambidextrous quadrant became the strategic agenda the roadmap sequences.

04Sensing and Responding Loop

The loop turned the day's insight into an organizational design question: who reads weak signals, how their time is protected, what the escalation path is, and who decides whether a response is warranted at what cost threshold. The current answers were nobody, nowhere, and by accident. That finding anchors the middle third of the roadmap.

05The Methuselah Principle

The day closed against the long horizon. Every decision made on the walk was held to the question the grove anchors: which of today's decisions will look obviously wrong in twenty years, and which features the organization is being told to smooth out may be the ones that carry it through. One such feature made the leverage inventory on that test.

The sequence matters. Signs without Teece is an interesting observation that fails to produce action. Each framework hands its output to the next, and run in order the five produce the four sections that follow.
