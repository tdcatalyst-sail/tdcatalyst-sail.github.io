How to choose a tier

Three prescriptive starting points.

Trail Diagnostic

Start here if you are evaluating the methodology, working through a specific near-term decision, or want a low-commitment entry point before engaging at deeper levels. The Trail Diagnostic qualifies fit on both sides.

Catalyst Field Session

Start here if you are actively leading an AI workforce transformation and want a full-arc diagnostic with priority actions in a single day of focused work. You have a clear challenge and want the full method applied to it now.

Catalyst Immersive

Start here if you are navigating a transformation that will unfold over months, where a single diagnostic engagement would not produce the depth of organizational design work needed. The challenge is live and complex.

Logistics

What to expect before, during, and after.

Location and meeting points

Mori Point trailhead, intersection of Bradford Way and Mori Point Road, Pacifica. Approximately 25 minutes south of San Francisco International Airport. For Full Day and Immersive, the afternoon portion is at the Methuselah grove on Skyline Boulevard, 25 minutes east of Pacifica.

Before the engagement

A 15-minute intake questionnaire one week before, followed by a 30-minute prep call the same week. The questionnaire surfaces the specific adaptive challenge and the context the day will work inside. Preparation is light by design.

During the engagement

Walks are deliberately slow. Anyone comfortable with a 3-mile walk on varied terrain can complete the day. Phones and laptops remain in the car during field portions. Emergency reachability is arranged in advance. Engagements proceed in fog, light rain, and typical coastal conditions. Heavy rain or high-wind warnings trigger a reschedule at no fee.

After the engagement

Written diagnostic delivered within 7 to 10 days, PDF by email, printed version on request. Length is 12 to 20 pages for Full Day and Immersive, proportionally shorter for Trail Diagnostic. Optional 45-minute review call for Full Day and Immersive.

What the diagnostic reads like

A redacted excerpt from a real engagement output.

The full sample is shared under NDA after the initial conversation.

Your organization is not failing to respond to AI. It is responding correctly to the wrong signal. The signal it is reading is efficiency pressure. The signal it is missing is a paradigm-level shift in customer expectations, a shift that is approximately 12 to 18 months ahead of the efficiency curve and will determine the competitive landscape for the next decade.
The central strategic question is not whether to deploy AI more aggressively. It is whether the three or four organizational features currently under pressure to be optimized away are in fact the capabilities that will define competitive advantage in the post-efficiency phase of the AI transition.
