AN ADVISORY PRACTICE BY THOMAS DELAPORTE

Adaptability is the leadership capability the AI Revolution demands.

The pace of a coastal walk changes what leaders notice and what they are willing to say out loud. Distance from the conference room is where complex organizational questions finally clarify. TDcatalyst advises senior executives on AI workforce transformation through a five-framework method, delivered in two specific landscapes on the Pacific coast and the Skyline ridge.

Read the Adaptability Framework Begin a conversation

Most organizations entering AI transformation are asking the wrong question. They are asking how to deploy AI effectively. The more consequential question is what kind of leadership AI makes necessary, and whether their people are capable of it.

The answer is not more technical literacy. It is a different cognitive posture: adaptability over optimization. As AI absorbs the performance layer of work, what remains irreducibly human is the adaptability layer, reading complex, ambiguous signals in real time and acting on them with complete ownership before the competition has noticed they exist.

That capacity does not develop in conference rooms. It develops in environments that demand it.

What every engagement produces

A written diagnostic. Four areas. Every finding specific.

Every engagement concludes with a structured written output delivered within 7 to 10 days. The format and depth vary by tier. The four areas are consistent across all tiers.

01

Named adaptive challenge

A precise, written statement of the gap between your organization's current posture and the leadership capability the AI transition demands of it. Named, not generalized.

02

Organizational landscape analysis

A structured read of the signals, assets, and constraints your operating environment is already producing. What your landscape is showing you, and what it is obscuring.

03

Leverage inventory

The specific capabilities, structural advantages, and resources available to close the identified gap. An honest accounting of what is present, what is absent, and what is misallocated.

04

90-day starting roadmap

A sequenced set of first moves with clear ownership and decision points. Built to act before the signal hardens, not after. PDF delivery within 7 to 10 days, 12 to 20 pages for a full-day engagement.
