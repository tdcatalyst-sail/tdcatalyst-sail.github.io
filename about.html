Operator-authored. One practitioner behind all three practices.

Most advisors on AI workforce transformation have worked one side of the problem. Tom Delaporte has worked three — AI product, enterprise consulting, and operator-side transformation — with commercial accountability throughout. Sail, Grow, and Advise are three ways into the same body of work.

What TDcatalyst brings

Three things that rarely come together in one practitioner.

A method sourced and tested

The frameworks draw on Dynamic Capabilities (Teece, UC Berkeley), Organizational Ambidexterity (O’Reilly and Tushman, Stanford and Harvard), and Sensing and Responding (Burnard and Bhamra). They are applied and refined through transformation work across Fortune 100 enterprises and public sector organizations, with measurable outcomes throughout.

An eight-figure track record

Every practice is delivered personally by someone who built a customer practice from a standing start into an eight-figure ARR business at an enterprise AI company, on consulting foundations from a global firm. The judgment behind Sail, Grow, and Advise is the same, sourced from the same work.

Operator accountability

The work does not produce reports for review. Every engagement ends with a written diagnostic, decisions made, or specific moves to make — and someone who owns them. Every recommendation is specific, traceable, and owned. The standard is the same standard applied when the work was being done from the inside, with a P&L attached.

Practice

Tom Delaporte founded TDcatalyst after building and running transformation programs at the intersection of AI product, enterprise consulting, and operator-side transformation — three domains that rarely meet in a single practitioner.

The work arrives in three forms, so there are three ways in. Sail frames the adaptive challenge with executives in the field. Grow executes the transformation inside the enterprise operating model. Advise counsels founders crossing into enterprise adoption. One seam, seen from three sides; the same judgment behind each.

Background

My career spans three areas that rarely meet: AI product, global change consulting, and HR strategy and operations. Leading an AI transformation means working the seams between them.

I led Talent Transformation, Talent Intelligence Platform, and Agentic Scale programs at an enterprise AI company, where I built a customer practice from a standing start into an eight-figure ARR business across Fortune 100 and public sector clients.

Before that, as an Engagement Manager at a global consulting firm in San Francisco, I led organizational design, change, and M&A integration engagements for global companies. And before consulting, I ran HR and transformation programs on the operator side, across France, Vietnam, the Philippines, China, and India.

The combination is not common. Most advisors have worked one side. I’ve worked three, with commercial accountability throughout. I’m bilingual in French and English with a working knowledge of Spanish, and have lived and worked on most continents. The ability to work well with people across cultures is how impact and outcomes materialize.
