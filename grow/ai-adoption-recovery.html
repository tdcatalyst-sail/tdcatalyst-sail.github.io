The Recovery Verdict.

Scale · Repair · Stop

One written document. Short enough to read, specific enough to act on, and built to survive being forwarded to someone who was not in the room.

  • Why it stalled. One cause, named, with the evidence behind it.
  • Whether it can earn its place. Scale, repair or stop, called on each workstream.
  • A 90-day plan. In order, with a named owner on every move.
