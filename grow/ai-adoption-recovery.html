How it works

AI-native diagnostics, simulation, and delivery.

The practice runs on its own instruments. They compress what internal teams spend months assembling into a ten-day engagement.

1 · Diagnose the pattern

MIT reports 95% of enterprise GenAI pilots show no measurable P&L impact. The causes are organizational, and they follow patterns. The diagnostic maps your rollout onto four researched archetypes.

Archetype 01

The Seam Organization

AI sits in one function. The rest were brought in too late.

Archetype 02

The Unmarked Passage

The change has no named identity. Leadership performs a transition it has not entered.

Archetype 03

The Operating Model Lag

The tools arrived. The work did not change.

Archetype 04

The Sensing Deficit

The board is asking questions the team cannot answer yet.
