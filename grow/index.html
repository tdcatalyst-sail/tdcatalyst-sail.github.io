The method behind the engagements

A fixed fee is possible because the method is already built.

The engagements are not assembled from scratch each time. Behind them sits a standing body of transformation work, drawn on and adapted to where your program is actually stalling: a library of interventions, the playbooks that sequence them, and the toolkits your own people keep.

A library of interventions

A curated set of adoption moves, each matched to a specific stall on the Adoption Matrix and grounded in a named behavioral framework: Diffusion of Innovations, COM-B, Prosci, Self-Determination Theory. Executive sponsorship, proof seeding, workflow redesign, manager enablement, sustainment. Diagnosis picks the few that move your population and sets the order they run in.

Playbooks that sequence them

Sequences built for the situations that keep recurring: an adoption reset after a launch that stalled, governance for working with vendor Forward Deployed Engineers, a reorganization transition that carries the key people with it. The order is where the value sits. Run the same interventions out of sequence and the change decays.

Toolkits your team keeps

The tangible pieces that stay after the engagement ends. Manager one-pagers and talking tracks, champion enablement kits, a whole-product kit of integrations, templates, and function-specific skill libraries, and measurement moved off training completion onto behavior change. Built to be owned and run internally.

The Transformation Dynamics Simulation™ is that library made interactive: sequence the interventions yourself and watch the adoption curve respond, including the point where change becomes self-sustaining.
