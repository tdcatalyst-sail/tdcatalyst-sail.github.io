TDcatalyst Grow · Deploy

Most organizations don't have an AI problem. They have a transformation problem.

The tools are deployed; the work looks the same. Deployment is a project plan. Transformation is a population of people choosing, every day, to work differently. That's where it stalls: adoption that peaks at launch and decays by quarter's end, forty pilots with no shared definition of done, an org chart built for work AI has already changed. Grow is fixed-scope consulting that turns deployment into transformation that holds: adoption that survives after the spotlight moves, a portfolio with the funding calls made, and an organization that matches the work. This is behavioral and organizational work, sequenced so the change compounds instead of decaying. It is not a communications campaign. Delivered personally by Tom Delaporte, who built an eight-figure workforce exchange practice at Eightfold AI, on consulting foundations from Capgemini.

The engagements

Three fixed-scope offers. Each delivers concrete decisions.

Most clients choose one, based on where the transformation is stalling. Every engagement is scoped in writing, priced before it starts, and delivered by Tom personally. No leverage pyramid, no junior team learning on your budget.

01

AI Portfolio Review · 30 days

$20K–$35K

Every AI initiative and embedded vendor FDE, inventoried and scored on value, risk, and readiness. Vendors deploy Forward Deployed Engineers faster than most organizations can manage them. You walk away with a scale / hold / kill call on every line item, a governance playbook for working with FDEs, and your team trained into a cadence that keeps pace. Decision-grade in thirty days.

If necessary, flows into an AI Deployments Lead engagement as ongoing advisory retainer.

02

Adoption Reset · 60–90 days

$40K–$75K

When a deployment launches and adoption stalls. Diagnosis by role and workflow, aligned incentives and policy, measurement moved from training completion to behavior change. Enablement runs through two workshops — AI Strategy for Leaders (half-day) and a two-day Adoption Enablement Workshop for managers and champions. You walk away with usage that holds past the launch quarter and your own managers and champions owning the reinforcement.

Range scales with the number of enablement workshops.

Try the AI Transformation Simulation →

03

Reorganization Blueprint · 6–10 weeks

$55K–$85K

When AI absorbs part of the work, roles and decision rights stop matching reality. You walk away with a target operating model, redefined roles and accountability maps, and a sequenced transition plan that brings the key people along.

Ongoing

Retained Advisory

A transformation doesn’t end at handoff — it has to be governed and reinforced while the internal teams carry it. The retainer is that steering layer: Tom stays engaged on a monthly cadence to keep the change compounding after the fixed-scope work lands.

  • Governance & risk monitoring — the portfolio governance model, decision rights, and policy guardrails held as models and regulation move.
  • Change sustainment — reinforcement so adoption doesn’t erode back to baseline; champion-network health and early warning when a segment stalls.
  • Value-realization measurement — real output tracked (speed, quality, results), not usage, so the investment keeps proving out.
  • Core-team enablement — periodic upskilling on new capabilities and plays, keeping the internal team ahead of vendor speed.
  • Portfolio, vendor & operating-model upkeep — fresh scale/hold/kill calls, vendor and FDE relationship governance, and role/decision-right adjustments as new initiatives land.
$6K–$10K / month
Six-month minimum
