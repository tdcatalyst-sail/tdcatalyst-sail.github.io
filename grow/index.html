Most organizations don't have an AI problem. They have a portfolio problem.

Forty pilots, three platforms, no shared definition of done. Adoption that peaks at launch and decays by quarter's end. An org chart designed for work AI has already changed. Grow is fixed-scope consulting that converts that picture into three outcomes: a portfolio with funding decisions made, adoption that holds after the spotlight moves, and an organization that matches the work. The engagements

Three fixed-scope offers. Each delivers concrete decisions.

Every engagement is scoped in writing, priced before it starts, and delivered by Tom personally — no leverage pyramid, no junior team learning on your budget.

01

AI Portfolio Review · 30 days

Every AI initiative in flight, inventoried and scored against value, risk, and readiness. You walk away with a scale / hold / kill recommendation for each line item, a sequencing roadmap for the next two quarters, and a board-ready readout. Decision-grade in thirty days.

02

Adoption Reset · 60–90 days

When a deployment launches and adoption stalls. Diagnosis by role and workflow, manager enablement, aligned incentives and policy, measurement shifted from training completion to behavior change. You walk away with usage that survives the quarter after launch — and the number that proves it.

03

Reorganization Blueprint · 6–10 weeks

When AI absorbs part of the work, roles and decision rights stop matching reality. You walk away with a target operating model, redefined roles and accountability maps, and a sequenced transition plan that brings the key people along.

Organizations that want continuity after a fixed-scope engagement retain Tom on a monthly advisory cadence — portfolio reviews, adoption checkpoints, and organizational calls as they arise.

Experimental · Embodied strategic framing

The hardest decisions rarely come clear at a conference table.
