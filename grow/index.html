TDcatalyst Grow · Enterprise & public sector

Most organizations don't have an AI problem. They have a portfolio problem.

Forty pilots, three platforms, no shared definition of done. Adoption that peaks at launch and decays by quarter's end. An org chart designed for work AI has already changed. TDcatalyst Grow is transformation consulting for enterprises and public-sector organizations that need their AI investments to behave like a managed portfolio, not a collection of experiments.

Services

Three bodies of work. One operating model.

Each can stand alone. Most engagements start with one and earn their way into the others, because the three problems are rarely separable for long.

01

AI deployment portfolio management

An inventory of every AI initiative in flight, scored against value, risk, and readiness. Sequencing and kill decisions made explicit. Governance that gives the board a portfolio view instead of a demo reel — what is deployed, what it returns, and what it costs to keep.

02

Change management & adoption

The distance between licensed and used is where AI value dies. Adoption architecture by role and workflow, manager enablement, incentive and policy alignment, and measurement that tracks changed behavior rather than completed training. Built to survive the quarter after launch.

03

Reorganization & transformation

When AI absorbs part of the work, roles, decision rights, and team boundaries stop matching reality. Organizational design for the operating model AI makes possible: spans and layers, new role definitions, accountability maps, and the transition plan to get there without losing the people who matter.

Who it's for

Leaders accountable for how AI enters the operating model.

The typical client is a transformation, AI, technology, or workforce leader at a mid-to-large enterprise or a public-sector organization — someone whose name is attached to the AI line in the budget and to the question the board asks next quarter. The work draws on programs delivered for Fortune 100 enterprises, the DoD Defense Innovation Unit, state labor departments, and global pharmaceutical companies.

How it works

Diagnose, decide, embed.

01

Diagnose

A structured assessment of the AI portfolio, the adoption reality on the ground, and the organizational friction between them. Findings in writing, every claim traceable. Typically two to four weeks.

02

Decide

A working session with the accountable leadership team to turn findings into a sequenced roadmap: what advances, what stops, who owns what, and what the next two quarters commit to.

03

Embed

An ongoing advisory cadence through execution — portfolio reviews, adoption checkpoints, and organizational decisions as they arise. Scoped monthly, scaled to the program, ended when the capability is internal.
