Instrument 02 · Vendor FDE governance

Twelve questions to ask before the engineers arrive.

Forward deployed engineers are the fastest way to get a deployment moving, and an unmanaged surface. They build in your environment, at their velocity, against their roadmap incentives. None of that is bad faith. It is the absence of a governance conversation that belongs in week one and usually happens in month nine. Ask these across the table, and write the answers down.

Visibility

  • 1. Where is the running inventory of everything you build here, and who on our side can read it without asking you?
  • 2. Which of our systems and data will you touch, and who on our side approves each new connection before it is made?
  • 3. What are you building this month, and in which meeting do we hear that before it starts rather than after it ships?

Decision rights

  • 4. What are you allowed to decide alone, what needs our named owner, and where is that line written down?
  • 5. When what you are building overlaps something our own team owns, who decides which version lives?
  • 6. When your roadmap and our priorities conflict, who do you escalate to on your side, and who is the counterpart on ours?

Ownership

  • 7. Who owns the code, the prompts, the configurations, and the integrations you build here if we part ways?
  • 8. Which of our people can maintain each thing you build, and what is the plan for making that true where it is not?
  • 9. What happens to everything you built when the contract ends: what transfers, in what form, with how much handover time?

Value

  • 10. What measure of our output, not your activity, will show this engagement worked, and when do we read it together?
  • 11. Which things you build are throwaway proofs and which are production, and who decides when one becomes the other?
  • 12. What would make you tell us to stop or scale down, and has your team ever said that to a client?

If more than four of these have no written answer, the portfolio needs a governance pass before the next initiative starts. That is the AI Portfolio Review: tdcatalyst.com/grow.
