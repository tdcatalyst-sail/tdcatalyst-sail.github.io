Instrument 01 · The Adoption Matrix

Your population is not one group. It is sixteen cells.

Rows are diffusion segments: who moves when, on any change. Columns are stages of change: where each person stands on this one. Adoption stalls cell by cell, and each cell responds to a different move. The full argument and sources are in the essay at tdcatalyst.com/sail/field-note-the-adoption-matrix.

Segment Precontemplation Contemplation Preparation Action / Maintenance
Innovators & Early Adopters
~16%		 Nobody has asked them. Sponsor reaches out directly. Ready to talk. Early access plus talking points for peers. About to start. Clear every obstacle; make week one a success story. Quiet experts. Make them visible: champion network, public credit.
Early Majority, the pragmatists
~34%		 No link to their job yet. A 15-minute peer call from their own function. Waiting for proof. Ship the whole product: toolkit, integrations, stories from their function. Willing but no cue. Give one: when X happens this week, do Y. Started, could drift. Manager check-ins, coaching circles, peer momentum.
Late Majority, the skeptics
~34%		 Waiting feels safe. Show the cost of sitting out. Unspoken doubts. A call with someone like them who moved past the same fear. Afraid of failing in public. Smallest first step, a buddy, a manager script. Fragile new habit. Visible credit, a word from the executive.
Laggards
~16%		 Resisting from fear. One-on-one only; find the real fear, address that. Wavering but wary. Manager and skip-level conversations, support named. Only with help at their side. Office hours, a pre-built path, reassurance. Moved, wants no attention. Quiet recognition, steady support.

Where campaigns lose. Most budgets go to the top-left cell and the top-right cell. Adoption is won or lost in the middle two rows, in the contemplation and preparation columns. That is where two-thirds of your population lives.
