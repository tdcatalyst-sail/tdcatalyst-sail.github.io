The through-line

Three positions on the same seam.

AI programs stall in the same place regardless of who runs them: at the seam between AI product, operations, and people. Executives feel it as a leadership question. Enterprises feel it as an adoption and structure question. Founders feel it as a sales and readiness question. It is one seam, seen from three sides.

Tom Delaporte has worked all three sides — AI product leadership at an enterprise AI company, organizational design and change consulting at a global firm, and operator-side HR and transformation across five countries — with commercial accountability throughout. The three practices exist because the work arrives in three forms. The judgment behind them is the same.
