The through-line

What connects them.

Every stalled AI program I have seen stalled in the same place: the seam where the AI product, the operation, and the people meet. Executives tend to meet that seam as a leadership question. Inside an enterprise it surfaces as stalled adoption. Founders run into it when enterprise buyers hesitate.

I have worked on each side of it, with commercial accountability throughout: AI product leadership at an enterprise AI company, organizational design and change consulting at a global firm, and operator-side HR and transformation roles across five countries. The work is packaged as three practices because that is how it arrives. Underneath, it is one discipline.
