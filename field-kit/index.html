The Field Kit

Tools from the field, free and usable today.

Four one-page instruments from the working library behind the TDcatalyst engagements. Each one is built to be printed, taken into a meeting, and filled in with your own numbers. No email address required.

The instruments

Print them, argue with them, keep them.

01

The Adoption Matrix worksheet

Map your own population onto the two axes, diffusion segment against stage of change, and find the cells where usage is actually stuck. Most rollouts spend on the loud cells and lose in the quiet ones. The worksheet makes the biggest cells impossible to ignore.

Open the worksheet Read the essay behind it
02

Twelve questions for a vendor's forward deployed engineer

Vendors embed engineers inside your teams, and they build at their velocity, against their roadmap. This is the governance conversation most organizations have six months too late, in one page, before the engineers arrive.

Open the questions
03

The manager talking track

Managers are the highest-leverage channel in any adoption campaign, and the least equipped. One page a line manager can read before the first team conversation about what AI changes: what to say, what they will be asked, and what not to promise.

Open the talking track
04

The stall diagnostic

Twelve questions, scored, on where your AI transformation is losing time. Interactive rather than printable, and the only instrument here that talks back. Results arrive as a written read of your stall pattern.

Run the diagnostic↗
These instruments come from the same library the paid engagements draw on. If one of them surfaces a problem bigger than a worksheet, that is what the engagements are for: Grow for the program, Sail for the leadership question underneath it.

If the worksheet named the problem, thirty minutes will scope the fix.
