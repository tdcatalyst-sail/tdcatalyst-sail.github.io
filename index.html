The through-line

A compounding practice.

Every stalled AI program I have seen stumbled in the same place: the seam where the AI product, the operations, and the people meet. Executives feel it as a leadership question. Enterprise ops teams feel it as an organizational systems, people change, and delivery question. Emerging founders feel it as an enterprise dynamics learning curve question.

I have worked on each side of it, with commercial accountability throughout: AI product leadership at an enterprise AI company, organizational design and change consulting at a global firm, and operator-side HR and transformation roles across five countries. The work is packaged as three practices because that is how it arrives. Underneath, it is one discipline.
