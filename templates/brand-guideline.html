The brand system.

One page for everything that keeps the site, the documents, and the decks reading as one practice. Trailhead direction, adopted July 2026.

01 · Color

Logo-exact, with one text exception.

Accents are sampled from the logo artwork's stroke cores. The single exception: logo gold fails accessibility contrast as small text on light grounds, so text and hover accents use a deepened tone of the same hue (--terracotta). Graphics — bars, rules, contours, buttons — always use the exact values.

02 · Type

Three faces, three jobs.

Besley · headings, logo, document titles
Advisory for the AI Revolution.

A Clarendon revival — the letterform of trail signage, USGS maps, and field guides. Weight 500 for headings, 700 for the TD lockup. Free on Google Fonts.

Archivo · body, UI, forms
AI programs stall on people and structure far more often than on the technology. That is the part I work on.

Body at 17.5px / 1.65. Weights 400 body, 500–600 emphasis and buttons. Free on Google Fonts.

IBM Plex Mono · eyebrows, captions, coordinates, data
37.61° N · 122.49° W · THE FIELD, PACIFICA COASTAL RIDGE

The surveyor's-notation register that pairs with the terrain device. Small sizes, generous letter-spacing, uppercase. Free on Google Fonts.

03 · The terrain device

Every artifact gets its own ground.

Generated topographic contours — thin lines in navy (or a practice tint), every fifth line heavier in the accent, like index contours on a real map. Rules:

  • It works the edges: heroes, covers, CTA panels, dividers, card bases. Never at high contrast behind running text.
  • Every page, document, and deck renders a unique seed — no ground repeats, the same rule as the photography.
  • Site alphas: hub hero 0.14/0.35, interior heroes 0.09/0.20, navy panels 0.35/0.5, card bases 0.09–0.13.
  • On the site it is drawn live by script.js (canvas.terrain with data-seed / data-line / data-index / data-alpha / data-index-alpha / data-fade); for documents and decks use the pre-rendered PNGs below.
04 · Google Slides

Deck setup, once per master.

Fonts. All three brand faces are native Google Fonts: in Slides, open the font picker → More fonts → add Besley, Archivo, and IBM Plex Mono. Titles Besley 500; body Archivo 400; labels/kickers IBM Plex Mono uppercase.

Backgrounds. Slide → Change background → Choose image → paste the asset URL:

  • deck-title-navy.png — title and closing slides (text in offwhite #F2F4F1, accents gold)
  • deck-divider-sail.png · grow · advise — section dividers, practice-colored
  • deck-content-light.png — content slides; terrain sits right, keep text left of center

Slide craft. Action titles (the takeaway as a full sentence, Besley), one idea per slide, mono kickers for section numbers, navy text on light grounds, chart colors from section 01 only.

Colors in Slides. Add the hex values from section 01 as custom theme colors so charts and shapes stay exact.

05 · Documents

Two templates, print to PDF.

Client-facing documents live as print-ready pages that share the site's stylesheet, so they can never drift off-brand. Open, click into the text to edit (highlighted spans are placeholders), then print → save as PDF (US Letter).

  • Diagnostic report — findings across the five frameworks + the 90-day roadmap
  • Engagement proposal — context, fixed scope, timeline, investment, terms, acceptance
06 · Voice

Tom's copy is canonical.

  • Styling changes never rewrite copy. Copy edits ship only with Tom's explicit sign-off; questions are batched, not improvised.
  • Claim first. Headlines state what the practice does; the thinking goes in the line below.
  • No em dashes in visible copy — colons, commas, full stops.
  • Vary sentence shape. No three parallel sentences in a row, with one standing exception: the executives / enterprises / founders triplet on the hub, kept by choice.
  • First person on the hub ("I work on…"); sub-pages keep their existing register.
  • Vocabulary: "the AI Revolution" (hub headline), "embodied strategic framing" (Sail's framing), "transformation consulting" (Grow — broader than deployment and adoption).
07 · Photography

Original frames, used once.

  • Each Pacifica frame appears exactly once site-wide; never reuse a frame on a second page, never swap in stock.
  • Caption venues accurately (Mori Point, Pedro Point Headlands, San Pedro Valley Park, the Methuselah grove) in the mono register, with coordinates where it fits.
  • A weak photo is worse than none — photo-free pages stay photo-free.
